# Governance

KubeRoot currently follows a **maintainer-led** model.

## Roles

- **Contributors**: anyone who opens issues, reviews, or submits pull requests.
- **Maintainers** (listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)): triage issues, review and
  merge pull requests, approve design proposals, and cut releases.

## Decisions

- Day-to-day changes are decided through pull request review.
- Significant changes go through a [design proposal](docs/proposals/) and are accepted when a
  maintainer approves and merges it after a reasonable comment period (at least 5 days).
- Architectural decisions are recorded as [ADRs](docs/adr/).
- When maintainers disagree, they aim for consensus; failing that, the lead maintainer decides.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors with a sustained record of quality contributions and reviews may be nominated
by an existing maintainer. Nomination is accepted by consensus of current maintainers.

This document will evolve as the community grows.
